  # Privacy Policy

  Last updated: August 14, 2026

  Training Timer Pro is developed by NordicAI Studio.

  ## Data Collection

  Training Timer Pro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

  The app does not require an account, does not use analytics, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not send timer or workout data to
  NordicAI Studio or to any third party.

  ## Data Stored on Your Device

  Training Timer Pro stores timer presets, custom workout programs, vibration settings, and notification sound settings locally on your device.

  This data remains on your device and is not accessible to NordicAI Studio.

  ## Permissions and Device Features

  Training Timer Pro may use vibration and notification sounds to alert you during workouts. These features are controlled by your device settings
  and the app settings.

  The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or health data.

  ## Data Sharing

  NordicAI Studio does not share user data with third parties because the app does not collect user data.

  ## Data Retention and Deletion

  Locally stored app data remains on your device until you edit it, reset it, clear the app data, or uninstall the app.

  Uninstalling Training Timer Pro removes the app’s locally stored data from your device, subject to your device operating system behavior and
  backups.

  ## Security

  Because Training Timer Pro stores data locally on your device, you are responsible for securing your device with appropriate system protections
  such as screen lock, device encryption, and operating system updates.

  ## Children’s Privacy

  Training Timer Pro is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

  ## Changes to This Privacy Policy

  NordicAI Studio may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
